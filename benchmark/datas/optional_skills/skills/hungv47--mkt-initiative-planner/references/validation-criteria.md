# Initiative Validation Criteria

Use this reference when reviewing initiative proposals. Each section covers what to look for, common problems, and questions to stress-test the initiative.

## Field-by-Field Validation Guide

### Name

**Quality Criteria**:
- Clear and memorable
- Describes what it does, not what it is
- Action-oriented or outcome-oriented

**Red Flags**:
- Internal jargon only the team understands
- Generic ("Q1 Growth Initiative")
- Too clever/cute to be clear

**Stress Test Questions**:
- Would a new team member understand what this is?
- Would you be embarrassed to present this name to leadership?
- Does the name set the right expectations?

---

### Type (Hero/Support)

**Quality Criteria**:
- Matches the scope and resources requested
- Appropriate for the expected impact
- Consistent with portfolio balance

**Red Flags**:
- Hero disguised as Support (underestimating resources)
- Support disguised as Hero (over-engineering)
- No clear rationale for classification

**Stress Test Questions**:
- If this takes 2x longer than expected, is it still worth it as a [type]?
- Could this be descoped to Support? Should it be upgraded to Hero?
- What happens to the portfolio balance with this classification?

---

### Hypothesis

**Quality Criteria**:
- Follows If/Then/Because structure
- Specific and testable
- "Because" cites evidence or insight
- Falsifiable (you can prove it wrong)

**Red Flags**:
- No "because" (just If/Then)
- Vague outcomes ("will improve")
- Multiple unrelated hypotheses bundled
- Unfalsifiable ("will contribute to growth")

**Stress Test Questions**:
- What data would prove this hypothesis wrong?
- Why do you believe the "because"? What's the evidence?
- If the outcome doesn't happen, what did you learn?

See [hypothesis-framework.md](hypothesis-framework.md) for detailed guidance.

---

### Growth Lever / Goal

**Quality Criteria**:
- Single, clear funnel stage
- Specific metric identified
- Connects to company-level goals

**Red Flags**:
- Multiple stages ("awareness and conversion")
- Generic ("growth")
- Vanity metric (followers, likes without connection to business)
- Metric not currently tracked

**Stress Test Questions**:
- If this metric improves but revenue doesn't, was it successful?
- Is this metric a leading or lagging indicator?
- Who else is trying to move this metric? Are you aligned?

---

### Segment

**Quality Criteria**:
- Clearly defined who's included
- Size estimated (even if rough)
- Eligibility criteria explicit
- Reachable through intended channels

**Red Flags**:
- "Everyone" or "all users"
- No size estimate
- Criteria that can't be operationalized
- Segment too small to matter or too large to target

**Stress Test Questions**:
- If you had to email this segment, could you pull the list?
- Is this segment worth the resources for its size?
- How does this segment differ from your average user?

---

### Mechanic

**Quality Criteria**:
- Step-by-step user journey documented
- Touchpoints and channels identified
- Clear call-to-action
- Dependencies on user behavior acknowledged

**Red Flags**:
- "And then they convert" without explaining how
- Missing steps in the journey
- Assumes behaviors without evidence
- No clear CTA

**Stress Test Questions**:
- Walk me through exactly what a user does, step by step
- Where is the highest drop-off risk?
- What if users don't behave as expected at step X?

---

### Budget

**Quality Criteria**:
- Itemized by category
- Realistic based on similar initiatives
- Contingency considered
- ROI expectations aligned with spend

**Red Flags**:
- Single lump sum with no breakdown
- Optimistic estimates ("should be around...")
- No contingency buffer
- Budget/impact ratio implausible

**Stress Test Questions**:
- What happens if costs run 50% over?
- How was this budget derived? Based on what?
- What's the minimum spend to learn if this works?

---

### KPIs

**Quality Criteria**:
- Single primary metric identified
- Guardrail metrics prevent gaming
- Baseline established
- Targets are stretch but achievable

**Red Flags**:
- Too many "primary" metrics
- No guardrails (could win metric by hurting something else)
- No baseline (how do you know if you improved?)
- Targets that are sandbagged or impossible

**Stress Test Questions**:
- If you could only track one number, which?
- What bad behavior could this metric incentivize?
- What's your confidence interval on the target?

---

### Dependencies

**Quality Criteria**:
- All required teams/partners listed
- Specific people identified, not just teams
- Status of each dependency known
- Approval path clear

**Red Flags**:
- "Engineering will help" (who specifically?)
- Assumed availability without confirmation
- No approval path for blockers
- External dependencies with no backup plan

**Stress Test Questions**:
- Have you confirmed availability with [dependency]?
- What if [dependency] is delayed by 2 weeks?
- Who can unblock you if you get stuck?

---

### Efforts

**Quality Criteria**:
- Hours/people estimated realistically
- Single owner named
- Team capacity verified
- Time accounted for in workplans

**Red Flags**:
- "Will figure it out"
- No owner named
- Assumed capacity without checking
- Initiative on top of already-full plates

**Stress Test Questions**:
- What is [owner] NOT doing to work on this?
- What happens if the owner is sick/leaves?
- Is this estimate based on similar past work?

---

### Impact Estimate

**Quality Criteria**:
- Both qualitative and quantitative
- Assumptions stated explicitly
- Confidence interval acknowledged
- Connected to business outcomes

**Red Flags**:
- "Significant improvement" with no numbers
- Precise numbers with no basis
- No assumptions stated
- Impact disconnected from company goals

**Stress Test Questions**:
- How did you derive that number?
- What's your confidence interval?
- What would need to be true for this estimate to be accurate?

---

### Confidence

**Quality Criteria**:
- Honest assessment (Low/Medium/High)
- Reasoning provided
- Evidence cited
- Aligned with risk tolerance

**Red Flags**:
- "High" with no justification
- Confidence inconsistent with evidence
- No reasoning provided
- Confidence changed to appease stakeholders

**Stress Test Questions**:
- What would make you change your confidence level?
- What would need to be true for this to fail?
- Are you confident because you have evidence or because you want it to work?

---

### Risks & Mitigations

**Quality Criteria**:
- Top 3-5 risks identified
- Each has likelihood and impact rated
- Mitigation plans are actionable
- Kill switch defined

**Red Flags**:
- Generic risks ("might not work")
- No mitigations (just listing risks)
- Mitigations that are other initiatives
- No kill switch (initiative runs forever)

**Stress Test Questions**:
- What's the worst-case scenario? How bad?
- For each mitigation: have you done this before?
- What would trigger you to kill this initiative?

---

### Timeline

**Quality Criteria**:
- Clear start and end dates
- Key milestones identified
- Buffer for unexpected delays
- Dependencies reflected in timeline

**Red Flags**:
- Open-ended ("ongoing")
- No milestones
- Timeline ignores dependencies
- No buffer (everything goes perfectly)

**Stress Test Questions**:
- What's on the critical path?
- What happens if you miss the first milestone?
- Is the end date a deadline or a guess?

---

### Success Criteria

**Quality Criteria**:
- Ship/iterate/stop thresholds defined
- Thresholds are specific numbers
- Connected to KRs or company goals
- Decision-maker identified

**Red Flags**:
- No kill criteria (can always find a reason to continue)
- Vague thresholds ("if it's working")
- Can be reinterpreted after the fact
- No connection to broader goals

**Stress Test Questions**:
- If you hit exactly the "iterate" threshold, what changes?
- Who decides ship vs kill? Is that clear?
- Could you move the goalposts after seeing results?

---

## Overall Initiative Health Check

### Coherence Test

Do all parts of the initiative align?
- [ ] Hypothesis → Mechanic (action matches hypothesis)
- [ ] Mechanic → KPIs (can measure the mechanic's effect)
- [ ] KPIs → Success Criteria (thresholds make sense for the KPIs)
- [ ] Budget → Impact (ROI is reasonable)
- [ ] Timeline → Dependencies (enough time for dependencies)

### Completeness Test

Are all fields filled in?
- [ ] All 15 fields have content
- [ ] No "TBD" or placeholder text
- [ ] Nothing obviously missing

### Reality Test

Does this feel realistic?
- [ ] Someone with context would believe this could work
- [ ] Resources requested match what's available
- [ ] Timeline is achievable given dependencies
- [ ] Impact estimate is plausible, not fantasy

### Learnability Test

Will you learn something regardless of outcome?
- [ ] Hypothesis is testable and falsifiable
- [ ] You'll know why it worked or didn't
- [ ] Failure modes are identified
- [ ] Next steps are clear for any outcome

---

## Validation Verdict Framework

Use this to summarize your assessment:

| Verdict | Criteria | Recommendation |
|---------|----------|----------------|
| **Ready to proceed** | All fields complete, coherent, realistic | Approve |
| **Needs minor revisions** | 1-2 fields weak, easily fixed | Approve with conditions |
| **Needs major revisions** | Multiple fields weak, or core hypothesis shaky | Return for rework |
| **Recommend rethinking** | Fundamental issues with premise or approach | Discuss alternatives |
